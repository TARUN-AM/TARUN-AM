### 1. SmrithiSangam — AI-Powered Cognitive Companion

**Helping people reconnect with meaningful memories through personalized, adaptive cognitive experiences.**

SmrithiSangam is an AI-powered cognitive companion designed to support people experiencing memory-related challenges, including people living with dementia. The project explores how personal memories, familiar experiences, and adaptive cognitive activities can be combined to create more meaningful and engaging digital experiences.

Rather than relying exclusively on generic memory exercises, SmrithiSangam focuses on making cognitive engagement more personal by connecting activities to an individual's life stories, familiar people, experiences, and cherished moments.

#### How It Works

- **Personalized Memory Experiences:** Uses meaningful personal memories and life experiences as the foundation for engaging cognitive activities.
- **Adaptive Cognitive Games:** Provides personalized games and exercises designed to encourage memory recall, attention, and continued mental engagement.
- **AI-Driven Personalization:** Explores how AI can help tailor activities and experiences to individual needs, preferences, and interactions.
- **Meaningful Engagement:** Aims to create opportunities for users to revisit familiar experiences and strengthen their connection with personal life stories.
- **Accessible Digital Experience:** Uses a mobile-oriented approach to make personalized cognitive activities more approachable for users and their caregivers.

### 2. CASENET — AI-Powered Criminal Network Intelligence

**Transforming fragmented criminal intelligence into connected, searchable, and actionable insights.**

CASENET is an AI-powered criminal network intelligence platform designed to help users explore relationships between criminal entities, investigate connections across information sources, and understand complex networks through structured data and intelligent retrieval.

Traditional investigations can involve large amounts of disconnected information about individuals, organizations, incidents, and their relationships. CASENET explores how Artificial Intelligence, knowledge graphs, and semantic search can help organize this information into a connected intelligence environment.

At its core, the platform brings together graph-based relationship analysis, backend intelligence services, and retrieval technologies to support more effective investigation and information discovery.

#### How It Works

- **Criminal Network Visualization:** Represents entities and their relationships as a connected graph, helping users explore links between people, organizations, incidents, and other relevant entities.
- **Knowledge Graph Analysis:** Uses Neo4j to model relationships and examine how entities are connected within a network.
- **AI-Powered Intelligence Retrieval:** Integrates AI-oriented retrieval capabilities to help users discover relevant information from available intelligence data.
- **Semantic Search:** Uses Qdrant to support vector-based retrieval, helping surface contextually relevant information beyond exact keyword matches.
- **Backend Intelligence Services:** Uses FastAPI to expose backend functionality and coordinate data processing and intelligence workflows.
- **Structured Data Management:** Uses PostgreSQL to store and manage structured information that supports the platform's analytical workflows.

#### Technology Stack

`Python` `FastAPI` `AI/ML` `Neo4j` `Qdrant` `PostgreSQL`

#### Project Links

- **Live Demo:** https://casenet-ai.onrender.com/
- **Source Code:** https://github.com/TARUN-AM/sih26189-criminal-network-intelligence

**Core idea:** Combining knowledge graphs, semantic retrieval, and AI-oriented analysis to make complex criminal network information easier to explore and understand.

*CASENET is an intelligence-support concept; the quality of its outputs depends on the available data, source reliability, and validation of inferred relationships.*
